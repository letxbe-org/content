type: definition
title: Cycle
---

Let $n \ge 1$ be an integer. A **cycle** is a list of integers between $1$ and $n$ that denotes a permutation of the [permutation group](@permutation-group) $S_n$.

The cycle $(a_1 \space a_2 \space ... \space a_m)$ sends $a_i$ to $a_{i+1}$ for $1 \le i \le m-1$ and sends $a_m$ to $a_1$. Every cycle represents some permutation in $S_n$.

The **length** of a cycle is the number of integers that which appear in it. A cycle of length $k$ is called a **$k$-cycle**. Two cycles are **disjoint** if they don't have no integers in common.

A **transposition** is a $2$-cycle.

Since $S_n$ is a [group](@permutation-group-is-group), the composition or **product** of two cycles also is a permutation of $S_n$.
