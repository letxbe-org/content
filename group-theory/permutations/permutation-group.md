title: Permutation Group
---

Let $X = \{1, 2, ..., n\}$ be the set of all natural numbers from $1$ up to $n$ for some integer $n \ge 1$. A **permutation** $\sigma$ on $X$ is a bijection $\sigma: X \to X$.

The **permutation group** $S_n$ is the [group](@group) of all permutations of $X$, together with _function composition_ $\circ$ as its [binary operation](@binary-operation). The [identity](@group) of the permutation group will be denoted by $\text{id}$.

In [@](!@permutation-group-is-group), we will prove that $S_n$ is indeed a group.
